Commercial Switcher

Detect and log only. Nothing is muted and Spotify is not touched. Turn off once the debug chart looks right.
In Spotify: … menu → Share → Copy Spotify URI (hold Option to see it). Used only with the playlist option.
Adds ~4 KB per second to the exported log (≈45 MB for a 3-hour game). Lets the sound banks be re-tuned offline from a live run. Takes effect the next time the extension is armed.
Keys must exist in the detector defaults. Leave empty for defaults.
